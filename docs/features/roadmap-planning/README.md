# Roadmap Planning

Turning a resolved roadmap into an approved plan of slices: what is taught, in what order, and
whether the learner builds it or a coding agent does.

## Epics

- **A roadmap resolves, and an interview establishes the learner's starting point and focus** — [#16](https://github.com/sameera/nexus-teach/issues/16) (filed in Nexus as #455)
- **Concepts are extracted per story, and every slice is marked learner or handoff** — [#17](https://github.com/sameera/nexus-teach/issues/17) (filed in Nexus as #456)
- **Ordering, splitting, scaffolds and the coverage check** — [#18](https://github.com/sameera/nexus-teach/issues/18) (filed in Nexus as #457)
- **The plan is approved at a decision-grade gate and renders as a home page** — [#19](https://github.com/sameera/nexus-teach/issues/19) (filed in Nexus as #458)
- **A stub's sources are pinned when its epic is promoted** — [#20](https://github.com/sameera/nexus-teach/issues/20) (filed in Nexus as #459)
- **A roadmap carries epics nobody has planned yet** — [#64](https://github.com/sameera/nexus-teach/issues/64)

## Notes

The first five were one epic stub — *A roadmap becomes an approved plan of lesson stubs*,
[#3](https://github.com/sameera/nexus-teach/issues/3) — before it was decomposed.

The home page #19 renders is a workbook page, so its concept files under
[Workbook Rendering](../workbook-rendering/README.md). The gate that produces the plan it draws
belongs here.

## Concepts

Fourteen pages, under the `roadmap-planning` domain in [the atlas](../../concepts.md) — the plan
contract itself at the parent, then `extraction`, `ordering` and `approval`.
